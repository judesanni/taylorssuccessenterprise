Funding opportunity · Atlanta, Georgia

A place to work.
A space to learn.
A source of opportunity.

A proposed Community Resource & Event Center designed to bring coworking, professional development, learning resources and event experiences together under one roof.

The concept

Business resources.
Community access.

The proposed center is designed to serve entrepreneurs, startups, small businesses, organizations, creatives and local residents with practical space and resources to help them move forward.

Its model combines a professional workspace with event and learning facilities, creating opportunities for people to work, meet, build skills, access technology and connect with the wider community.

The initial plan is to establish an Atlanta flagship, validate demand and operations, then assess future growth based on performance and community needs.

A proposed Atlanta flagship

Five connected pillars.

Flexible resources designed to support both business development and community learning.

Proposed funding request

$150,000

Funding sought to establish the proposed Community Resource & Event Center in Atlanta.

The planned use of funds includes facility build-out, technology infrastructure, furnishings, coworking and office fit-out, meeting rooms, event equipment, learning resources, initial staffing, marketing and working capital.

The proposed funding will support the initial location, core services and launch of the center.

The vision

An Atlanta flagship with room to grow.

The first goal is to establish a stable, useful and financially sustainable location before considering additional markets.

Success means more than opening a facility. It means building a reliable service model, earning community trust, supporting local enterprise and using evidence from actual operations to guide future decisions.
