Privacy Policy

This policy explains how Taylor Success Enterprise handles information when you browse this website or send an inquiry.

Last updated: October 23, 2026

Who we are

Taylor Success Enterprise is an Atlanta-based public relations, branding, marketing, and event production business. For privacy questions or requests, contact tsenterprisesatlanta@gmail.com or call (443) 347-3239.

Information we collect

If you use the contact form, we may collect the details you choose to provide, including your name, email address, phone number, company or organization, inquiry category, timeline, budget range, project or event name, message, referral source, and whether you opted in to marketing updates. Please do not submit sensitive financial information, government identification numbers, health information, passwords, or confidential third-party information.

Like most websites, the hosting and delivery systems may process basic technical information such as IP address, browser, device, request time, and pages requested for security, diagnostics, and delivery. The current site does not intentionally run an advertising pixel or analytics tag.

How we use information

We use inquiry details to review and respond to your request, communicate about potential work, coordinate follow-up, maintain business records, prevent spam or misuse, and meet legal obligations. We send marketing updates only if you separately choose the optional marketing checkbox; you can ask to stop those updates at any time.

Submitting a form does not automatically create a client relationship, guarantee availability, or commit either party to a project.

Service providers and sharing

The website is hosted through Netlify. When you submit a form, the information is processed by Netlify Forms and may be stored in the site's Netlify dashboard; authorized site administrators can access submissions. If email notifications are configured, submission details may also be sent to the designated business inbox. Netlify's own privacy and security terms apply to its processing.

The site loads some images from Unsplash and fonts from Google Fonts. Those services may receive technical information such as your IP address and browser request when your browser retrieves those resources. Links to Instagram, Eventbrite, and other third-party websites take you to services governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control their practices.

We do not sell personal information collected through this website. We may disclose information to service providers needed to operate the site, when you request it, when required by law, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, safety, or the integrity of the service.

Retention and security

We keep inquiry information for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, manage business relationships, maintain records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable obligations. Actual retention may also depend on the settings and retention features available in the hosting and email services. No website or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure; please avoid sending sensitive information through the form.

Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you submitted, or to withdraw from optional marketing communications. We may need to verify your identity and may retain some information where permitted or required by law. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under applicable privacy law. Contact tsenterprisesatlanta@gmail.com to make a request.

Children and policy updates

This website is intended for a general business audience and is not designed to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has submitted information, contact us so we can review it.

We may update this policy when the website, services, legal requirements, or data practices change. The updated date at the top of this page indicates the latest revision.

This page is a practical starting point for the current website setup, not legal advice. The business should review it with a qualified attorney before relying on it as a complete compliance document, especially if its services, data collection, tracking technologies, or operating locations change.
